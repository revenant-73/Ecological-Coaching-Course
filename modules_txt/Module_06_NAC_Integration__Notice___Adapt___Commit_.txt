### NAC: Your Real-Time Coaching Operating System

By now you've learned the concepts.

But in the moment—mid-practice, match pressure—you need a **decision-making loop** that's automatic.

That loop is NAC.

It's not a script. It's a compass.

### The Three Moves

--- NOTICE ---
What is actually happening?

Not what *should* happen.
Not what you *want* to happen.

**Actual observation:**
- Are passers moving forward or back?
- Are setters decision-ready or reactive?
- Is intensity rising or dropping?

Notice without judgment. Just data.
------------------

--- ADAPT ---
Change one constraint.

Not the game. Not the rules.
One **specific element** that addresses what you noticed:

- Change scoring
- Change contact limits
- Change court positioning
- Change rally structure
- Change your silence (or your presence)

One change. Watch what happens.
------------------

--- COMMIT ---
Don't revert immediately.

Let the constraint work for 5–10 minutes. Let players adapt. Watch if the behavior changes.

If it does → You've found something. Build on it.
If it doesn't → Different constraint next time.

No permanent decisions. Just iteration.
------------------

### Real-World NAC Examples

--- SCENARIO: Flat Energy ---
**Notice:** Players are going through motions. Low intensity.

**Adapt:** Change scoring. Winning team stays on court. Losing team sits out. (Suddenly every point matters.)

**Commit:** Run this for 10 minutes. Watch energy return. Now you know what drives engagement for this team.
------------------

--- SCENARIO: Setters Panicking ---
**Notice:** Setters are making poor decisions under pressure. High error rate.

**Adapt:** First contact to set = max 2 touches. (Forces faster decision-making in safer context.)

**Commit:** Run this until decision-making improves. Then gradually add complexity.
------------------

--- SCENARIO: Players Not Reading Blocks ---
**Notice:** Hitters are swinging into obvious blocks. No shot selection.

**Adapt:** Blocker can only see the setter, not the hitter. (Blocker can't cheat toward the line or angle.)

**Commit:** Run 15 minutes. Hitters must now read spacing and make actual decisions.
------------------

### REFLECTION: PRACTICE NAC THIS WEEK
Questions/Prompts:
1. At your next practice, pick one thing to NOTICE
2. Make one constraint change (ADAPT)
3. Let it run for 10 minutes without reverting (COMMIT)
4. What happened?

Closing Instruction: NAC only works when you use it. Start small.
