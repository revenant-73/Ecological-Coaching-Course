### You Don't Need New Drills

You don't need better speeches.
You don't need more conditioning.

You need one intentional change.

That's it. That's where learning starts.

--- YOUR IMMEDIATE CHALLENGE ---
At your next practice:

1. **Design one game** (doesn't have to be new—use your anchor game)
2. **Apply one constraint** based on something you saw in the past week
3. **Say nothing about technique.** Let the game teach.
4. **Ask one question afterward:**
   > "What did you notice changed?"
5. **Then stop talking.**
------------------

--- NOTHING ELSE ---
Don't try to implement everything.
Don't overhaul your entire practice.
Don't make five changes at once.

One constraint.
One game.
One practice.

Then you'll know if this works for your team.
------------------
